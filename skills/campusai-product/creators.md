# Who we learn from

A trending list is filtered by popularity. A person with taste is filtered by taste. Almost every
genuinely good thing we have added to CampusAI came from one of these people, so follow them,
save what you like, and send Jacob anything good.

When one of them publishes a skill, we read it and take the good ideas into our own process. We
do not pile up everyone's skills side by side, because skills that overlap fight each other.

## Using them

Your feed becomes your inspiration. Following these people on X and Instagram is what turns a
generic feed into a stream of good ideas. When something stops you scrolling, save it and ask:
what exactly makes this good, and where in CampusAI would it help a student?
